Open Call

Art + Technology

SOHMHOS x metanoIA
Feb 1–25, 2026

Open call for artists, collectives, or companies from any discipline developing projects at the intersection of art and technology. Selected proposals will be part of a physical exhibition in Monterrey on March 15, 2026. The deadline to submit proposals is Feb 25, 2026.

THEME: METANOIA

Metanoia explores processes of transformation that emerge when technology intervenes, mediates, or modifies the human experience.

Proposals should address, explicitly or implicitly, one or more of the following axes:

  • Human–technology interfaces.
  • Transformation, adaptation, or change induced by digital or computational systems.
  • Surveillance, observation, and privacy.
  • Human identity in a digital world.
  • Memory, documentation, and data accumulation.
  • Automation and technological dependence.
CONTEXT

Sohmhos develops projects that combine art, technology, and user experience. This open call prioritizes works that propose a direct relationship between human, space, and technology, using technology as part of the work’s language—not only as technical support.

We are looking for proposals that integrate analog and digital processes and establish direct interaction with the audience.

Finished works and projects in development are accepted, as long as they are feasible to exhibit within the event date and venue.

Open format.

SELECTION CRITERIA

Proposals will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

  • Conceptual clarity and relationship to the Metanoia theme.
  • Effective integration of analog and digital elements.
  • Interaction system design.
  • Technical and operational feasibility within the exhibition space.
TERMS
  • Applying to the open call is free.
  • Sohmhos does not offer fees, payments, or prizes for selected works.
  • Up to 5 works will be selected to be part of the exhibition.
  • Works must be original and may have been exhibited previously at another event.
  • Works may be offered for sale under the conditions of the event organized by Sohmhos.
  • Installation, operation, and de-installation are the responsibility of the selected artists.
  • Artist presence is not mandatory. However, it is recommended that at least one person responsible for the project be available during exhibition hours to ensure proper operation and to provide context to the audience.
  • The venue will provide basic electrical supply. Any additional technical requirements must be provided by the artist or responsible team.
  • Sohmhos is not responsible for any damage, loss, or malfunction that may occur during installation, exhibition, or de-installation.
  • It is recommended that the value of the work does not exceed $50,000 MXN, considering the venue conditions and the nature of the event.
  • Each work must fit within a maximum footprint of 1.20 m × 1.20 m × 2 m (width × depth × height).
